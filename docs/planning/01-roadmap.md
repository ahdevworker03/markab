# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the Vehicle Rental Management Platform. It provides a strategic direction for the product while allowing flexibility as customer feedback and market needs evolve.

The roadmap is not a fixed delivery schedule. Instead, it outlines the major stages of the product's growth and the objectives of each stage.

---

# Current Status

## Frontend MVP ✅

The project currently includes a validated frontend MVP that demonstrates the core product vision.

Achievements:

- Core user interface completed.
- Product validated through discussions with vehicle rental business owners.
- Initial business requirements collected.
- Product documentation established.
- Product direction confirmed as a SaaS platform.

Current objective:

Transform the validated prototype into a production-ready application.

---

# Phase 1 — Production Foundation

## Goal

Build the first production-ready version of the platform.

Primary objectives:

- Backend development.
- PostgreSQL database.
- Authentication and authorization.
- Multi-organization support.
- Core rental workflows.
- Vehicle management.
- Customer management.
- Maintenance management.
- Financial tracking.
- Dashboard.
- Offline synchronization.
- Responsive experience across desktop and mobile.

Success Criteria:

Businesses can replace their manual rental management process with the platform.

---

# Phase 2 — Production Launch

## Goal

Deploy the platform for real business usage.

Primary objectives:

- Production deployment.
- User onboarding.
- Initial business adoption.
- Bug fixing.
- Performance improvements.
- Customer support.
- Continuous feedback collection.

Success Criteria:

Businesses actively manage their daily rental operations using the platform.

---

# Phase 3 — Product Improvement

## Goal

Refine the platform based on real-world usage.

Primary objectives:

- Improve usability.
- Simplify workflows.
- Optimize performance.
- Improve offline synchronization.
- Expand reporting.
- Strengthen security.
- Improve mobile experience.

Success Criteria:

Customers rely on the platform daily and provide positive feedback regarding reliability and ease of use.

---

# Phase 4 — Platform Expansion

## Goal

Expand the platform while remaining focused on vehicle rental businesses.

Potential additions:

- Vehicle sales management.
- Buyer management.
- Vehicle lifecycle tracking.
- Online reservations.
- Customer portal.
- Advanced analytics.
- Accounting integrations.

Success Criteria:

The platform manages the complete lifecycle of business vehicles, from acquisition to rental and eventual sale.

---

# Phase 5 — SaaS Growth

## Goal

Scale the platform as a mature SaaS product.

Potential objectives:

- Subscription management.
- Automated billing.
- Enhanced organization management.
- Advanced user roles.
- Business customization.
- Multi-language support.
- Regional expansion beyond Lebanon.
- Operational monitoring and analytics.

Success Criteria:

The platform supports a growing number of independent vehicle rental businesses while maintaining reliability, security, and ease of use.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to become the preferred management platform for independent vehicle rental businesses.

The product should remain focused on solving operational challenges faced by rental businesses while expanding naturally into related capabilities such as fleet vehicle sales and advanced business management.

Growth should always be driven by validated customer needs rather than feature quantity.

---

# Roadmap Principles

Every future decision should follow these principles:

- Solve validated customer problems first.
- Keep the product focused on vehicle rental businesses.
- Prefer simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
- Build scalable solutions that benefit every customer.
- Maintain a single shared SaaS platform.
- Design for long-term maintainability.
- Expand only when there is clear business value.
- Continuously learn from customer feedback before introducing major features.

---

# Living Roadmap

This roadmap is intended to evolve alongside the product.

As new customer insights are collected and market conditions change, priorities may be adjusted. Every significant roadmap decision should remain aligned with the Product Vision and continue supporting the long-term goal of building a reliable, scalable, and customer-focused SaaS platform.
